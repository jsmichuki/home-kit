**Audience:** Engaged subscribers who are either first-time/recent homeowners or established homeowners. Exclude customers who already own the complete system.

**Campaign URL:** `https://astralrefine.com/?selection=complete&utm_source=email&utm_medium=campaign&utm_campaign=complete-new-homeowner-system`

## Five problem-led subject lines to test

- Your home did not come with an owner's manual
- What matters after closing
- The house always has a next thing
- Before the next repair becomes urgent
- Where did you put that warranty?

These subjects sell the recognizable problem—lack of order, scattered records, and reactive repairs—rather than leading with the product or price. Test only two or three at a time, with purchases per delivered email as the deciding metric.

## Send notes

- Segment by homeowner tenure: new/recent owners receive Variation A or B; established owners receive Variation C or D. Suppress complete-system customers and unsubscribed or bounced contacts.
- Test two or three subjects within each segment. Choose the winner on completed purchases per delivered email, not opens alone; use opens only as a directional early signal.
- Keep the campaign to one primary link. The landing URL opens the complete set already selected.
- Confirm SPF, DKIM, DMARC, mailing address, and unsubscribe-link merge field before sending.
