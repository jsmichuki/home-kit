# Complete New Homeowner Bundle — Product Structure & Packaging Blueprint

## 1. Product Concept

### Working product name
**The Complete New Homeowner System**

### Core promise
A practical, organized system that helps a recent homebuyer:

- Set up their new home correctly
- Keep up with maintenance
- Organize important home information and documents
- Budget for ownership costs and repairs
- Prepare for emergencies
- Hire and manage contractors
- Plan renovations and improvements

The product should feel like an **owner's manual for a house**, not a collection of unrelated ebooks.

### Recommended positioning
> **Your home didn't come with an owner's manual. This is it.**

The product should be organized around five simple homeowner jobs:

1. **SET UP** — get the home organized after closing
2. **MAINTAIN** — keep the property in good condition
3. **PROTECT** — prepare for emergencies and preserve important records
4. **BUDGET** — understand and plan homeownership costs
5. **IMPROVE** — plan repairs, contractors and renovations

---

# 2. Recommended Product Architecture

Do not create one 150-page PDF.

Instead, build the product as a **modular library** of focused PDFs plus editable spreadsheets/templates.

This has several advantages:

- Customers can buy only what they need.
- The checkout can support à la carte purchases.
- Individual modules create multiple email marketing angles.
- The complete bundle becomes an obvious upsell.
- Customers are less overwhelmed.
- You can update one product without regenerating the entire bundle.
- Future personalized versions can replace individual modules.

## Recommended launch structure

### PDFs
1. First 30 Days in Your New Home
2. First-Year Home Maintenance System
3. Home Safety & Emergency Binder
4. Homeowner Budget & Repair Planning Guide
5. Contractor Hiring & Home Repair Toolkit
6. Home Renovation & Improvement Planner
7. Home Records, Appliances & Warranty Organizer
8. Seasonal Home Care Guides

### Editable spreadsheets/templates
1. Homeownership Budget & Expense Tracker
2. Maintenance Calendar & Task Tracker
3. Home Inventory & Warranty Tracker
4. Contractor Quote Comparison & Project Tracker
5. Renovation Budget & Project Planner
6. Home Improvement Priority Planner

The full bundle therefore contains:

- **8 PDF guides/workbooks**
- **6 editable spreadsheets**
- Printable checklists
- Digital worksheets
- Bonus quick-reference sheets
- Optional personalized first-year plan

# 3. How the Customer Should Buy It

Yes — the PDFs can absolutely be sold using a **checkbox-style build-your-own bundle**.

This is a strong model because every selected module adds to the cart total while the customer can also choose the complete package.

## Recommended checkout model

Display the products as cards with checkboxes:

```text

[ ] Safety & Emergency Binder                  $15

----------------------------------------------

[ ] Get the Complete New Homeowner System      $69

Includes all 8 guides + all spreadsheets +
all printable templates + bonuses.
```

## Important recommendation

Do **not** sell the PDF and its related spreadsheet independently at first.

Instead, make each checkbox a **module**.

For example:

### Budget & Repair Planning Kit — $19
Includes:

- Homeowner Budget PDF
- Monthly expense spreadsheet
- Annual cost tracker
- Emergency repair reserve calculator

This feels more valuable and makes the catalog easier to understand.

# 5. Dynamic Bundle Pricing Strategy

The checkout should encourage customers to upgrade naturally.

For example:

```text
Your selections: $54

For just $15 more, unlock the COMPLETE bundle:
+ 4 additional guides
+ 3 additional spreadsheets
+ all seasonal checklists
+ homeowner records system

[Upgrade to Complete Bundle — $69]
```

This is better than simply showing a discount.

## Recommended rule

If the user's à la carte cart reaches approximately **65–75% of the full bundle price**, show an upgrade message.

Example:

```text
You've selected $51 worth of tools.

Upgrade to the complete $69 bundle for only $18 more.
```

If their individual selections exceed the bundle price, automatically replace the cart price with the lower complete-bundle price.

# 22. Personalization Architecture

Do not allow an AI model to invent maintenance rules.

A stronger architecture is:

```text
Home profile
     ↓
Rules/maintenance database
     ↓
Relevant task selection
     ↓
Schedule generation
     ↓
AI rewrites/explains the selected recommendations
     ↓
Personalized PDF
```

The maintenance recommendations should come from a controlled rules/data layer.

AI can help with:

- Explanation
- Friendly wording
- Summaries
- Personalized introductions
- Prioritization language
- Formatting

> **PDF = learn + check**

# 37. Key Recommendation

The checkbox idea is worth implementing.

However, present customers with **modules**, not individual raw files.

The user should think:

This keeps the storefront simple while preserving the flexibility of build-your-own pricing.
