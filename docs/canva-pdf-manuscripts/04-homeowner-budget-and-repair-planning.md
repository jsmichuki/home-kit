## Pages 2–3 — The real cost of homeownership

Your mortgage payment matters, but it is only one line in the ownership picture. A good plan makes room for predictable costs, irregular costs, and the surprises that come with a real building.

### The full picture

| Monthly or regular | Annual or irregular | Future or occasional |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage, utilities, HOA, routine services | Taxes, insurance changes, seasonal care, service visits | Repairs, appliance replacement, landscaping, renovations |

This is a planning guide, not financial advice. Use your own cash flow, loan terms, local tax/insurance information, and professional advice where needed. The goal is awareness—not a universal number.

## Pages 11–13 — Repair reserve planning

A repair reserve is a dedicated pool for the home’s maintenance and repair needs. It is not a prediction that something will fail next month. It is a way to give future-you options.

### Choose a method that you can sustain

- **Fixed contribution:** set aside a consistent amount each month.
- **Percentage approach:** use a percentage of income or property value only if it suits your financial plan.
- **Known-cost approach:** save toward identified maintenance or replacement needs.
- **Windfall approach:** direct part of irregular income, refunds, or bonuses when appropriate.

## Pages 18–20 — Emergency, necessary, or planned?

Use this decision frame before spending, while remembering that safety concerns and active damage deserve prompt professional help.

| Type | A practical definition | Your next move |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | Immediate risk to people, active major damage, or loss of essential service in unsafe conditions | Protect people/property, document, contact appropriate help. |
| Necessary repair | Problem that should be addressed soon to prevent damage, loss of function, or higher cost | Gather facts, set a timeline, obtain qualified quotes if useful. |
| Preventive work | Work that reduces the chance or impact of future damage | Schedule, budget, and track it. |
| Improvement | Comfort, efficiency, layout, or appearance change | Define success, savings target, and project scope before buying. |
