# Home Records, Appliances & Warranty Organizer

> **The permanent record for the home you own and the things that keep it running.**

## Design brief

**Suggested length:** 24–30 pages.  
**Visual mood:** archival yet approachable; labeled folders, thoughtful documentation, a sense of control.  
**[IMAGE DIRECTION — COVER]** Beautiful organized home office shelf with neutral folders, appliance manuals, a tablet, and a small house blueprint; calm, premium, no corporate logos.

## Pages 2–3 — Your home’s record belongs in one system

The most useful records are the ones you can retrieve in minutes. This organizer captures the home’s identity, major systems, appliances, improvements, warranties, service history, and household inventory.

**Keep:** a secure digital folder, a small physical file for originals, and a habit of photographing receipts and labels.

## Pages 22–24 — Important documents index

### What to save

- Purchase, closing, inspection, appraisal, survey, and property records
- Mortgage, tax, HOA, and insurance documents
- Appliance manuals, receipts, labels, warranties, and service invoices
- Contractor quotes, permits, approvals, final invoices, and before/after photos
- Home inventory photos and valuation records
