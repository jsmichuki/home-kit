## Shared design language

**Tone:** calm, capable, reassuring, practical—not alarmist or overly technical.  
**Visual style:** warm editorial home photography, generous white space, soft neutral backgrounds, charcoal body type, one muted accent color per homeowner job (Set Up, Maintain, Protect, Budget, Improve).  
**Safety note:** Use the recurring footer: *“This guide is for planning and recordkeeping. Follow appliance manuals and local requirements; when safety is in doubt, contact a qualified professional or emergency service.”*  
**Worksheet convention:** Leave generous writable space in print layouts. In digital layouts, use form-like lines, cards, or tables.
